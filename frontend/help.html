01

Getting started

What is Lèi?

Lèi is a community space for women to connect, share, discover interest-based groups, and find events. The public site offers a preview; member spaces require an account.

Who can join Lèi?

Lèi is designed for women, including cis and trans women. During sign-up, you can choose a space and review the community expectations before continuing.

How do I create and verify an account?

Choose Get Started on the home page and follow the sign-up steps. The current flow asks for account details and phone verification; complete the prompts shown in the app. If a code does not arrive, check your number and contact support.

Can I browse Lèi without creating an account?

Yes. You can explore the public Features and Community preview pages. Member conversations and member-only spaces are not available in the public preview.

02

Your account

How do I update my profile or profile picture?

Sign in and open your profile or account settings to see the options currently available. If a profile detail cannot be changed in the app, email support with the account email and the change you need.

How do I change my email or reset my password?

If you cannot find an email or password option on the sign-in screen, contact support from the form below. Never include your password or verification code in a support request.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account Settings and choose Delete Account if that option is available to you. If you cannot access the setting, send an account-access request below. Do not send your password or verification code.

03

Communities

How do I discover or join a community?

Explore the community spaces available after signing in. Public group previews are examples; availability and access can depend on the space and its membership settings.

Can I create a group or leave one?

Use the group controls shown in your signed-in space. If a control is missing or a group is inaccessible, contact support and include the group name and what you were trying to do.

How do I manage messages or report a conversation?

Use the conversation controls available in the signed-in app. For a safety concern, use Report a Member in the app when available, or choose the safety contact option below.

04

Events

How do I find events?

Browse the Events area in the signed-in app. The public Community page contains sample event previews, not live listings or confirmed attendee information.

How do I RSVP or cancel an RSVP?

Open the event and use the RSVP controls shown there. If your RSVP will not update or you need help with a cancellation, contact support with the event name.

Who can see that I am attending?

Check the event details and your current account privacy settings before sharing attendance information. Contact support if you need help understanding a specific event's visibility.

05

Safety & privacy

Your comfort matters. Safety concerns have a direct route to the team.

Who can see my profile and posts?

The sign-up flow includes public and private account choices, and Account Settings includes a profile visibility control. Review your current selection before posting. Dedicated privacy documentation is not published yet, so contact support with questions about a specific item.

How do I report a member or inappropriate behavior?

When signed in, open the profile menu and choose Report a Member. Share what you observed, including relevant context. Do not publicly identify or confront someone you are reporting. You can also email a safety concern below.

What happens after I submit a report?

Member reports are submitted privately to the Lèi team for review when report intake is available. Do not include passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary sensitive information. The Help Center cannot provide a case-status lookup yet.

How do I block someone or control who can message me?

Use the member and message controls available in your signed-in space. If you cannot find a control, contact support and describe what you need. The exact controls may vary as Lèi develops.

Policy pages are being prepared.

Community Guidelines, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and a full Safety Guide are not published yet. For urgent questions about how a feature works, contact the team before sharing sensitive information.

06

Technical support

I am not receiving my verification code. What should I do?

Confirm that the phone number is correct and can receive messages, then try the verification step again. If the issue continues, contact support and include your device type and the approximate time you tried. Do not email your code.

A page is not loading or the site looks wrong.

Refresh the page, check your connection, and try a current version of your browser. If the issue persists, send the page address, your browser and device, and the steps that led to the problem.

How do I report a bug?

Use the contact form below and choose Technical issue. Describe what you expected, what happened instead, and the browser and device you use. You can select a screenshot; your email app will ask you to attach it to the draft manually.

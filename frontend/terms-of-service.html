Terms of Service

Draft for review · October 2026

Draft, not final terms. This working draft is not a contract or approved terms of service. Eligibility, service operation, moderation, liability, dispute handling, and other terms require legal review before launch.

About this draft

These draft terms are intended to outline topics that final Lèi terms may address. They do not create or replace an agreement. Do not rely on this page as a complete statement of your rights or obligations.

Accounts and access

Lèi currently presents account registration, phone verification, and community-space selection as part of its sign-up experience. Eligibility rules, account security responsibilities, account recovery, and the process for closing an account need to be clearly defined in final terms.

Member contributions

Members may be able to create profile information, posts, messages, event details, and other material. Final terms should explain ownership, permissions needed to operate the service, content visibility, removal, and how member content is handled when an account closes.

Acceptable use and moderation

Members are expected to follow the Community Guidelines and any rules shown for specific spaces. The grounds for moderation, available actions, review process, and appeals need to be finalized and communicated before launch.

Service availability and changes

Features may change as Lèi is developed. Final terms should describe service availability, maintenance, changes or discontinuation, and any limitations or warranties that apply.

Privacy and contact

Information handling is addressed in the draft Privacy Policy. For current support options, visit the Help Center. The contact email presently displayed is a placeholder that must be replaced before launch.

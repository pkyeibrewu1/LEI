About Lèi

A space made for women to connect, share, and belong.

Lèi brings together the things we love about being online: a place to express yourself, connect with others, discover new things, and build communities of your own.

You should be able to share your thoughts, your life, and your experiences without feeling like you’re being reduced to how you look. You should be able to meet women who understand you, find people who share your interests, and create a community that feels like your own.

What members can do

  • Share thoughts, stories, opinions, and everyday moments.
  • Connect with women who share your interests, experiences, and goals.
  • Discover groups, events, conversations, and communities.
  • Support each other through wellbeing, safety, and mutual-aid resources.
  • Create spaces and communities of your own.

Lèi is about having a place to be yourself, meet your people, and make space for others too.

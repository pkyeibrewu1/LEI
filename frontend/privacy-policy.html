Safety & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Draft for review · October 2026

Draft, not final policy. This working draft is not a complete privacy notice and has not been approved for launch. Data practices, vendors, retention periods, and user rights need review and confirmation before publication.

Information you may provide

Depending on the features you use, Lèi may ask for details such as your name, username, email address, phone number, college or university, profile details, and information you choose to post or submit. Do not include passwords or verification codes in support or report messages.

Verification and service providers

Account setup includes phone verification. A verification request uses the phone number and code needed for that step and may be handled by an external messaging provider. The provider, data handling terms, and retention details must be confirmed before launch.

Reports and support requests

Member or problem reports can include a report category, details, contact email, and page address. If report storage is configured, reports are sent to the Lèi team’s report service for review. Support requests from the Help Center prepare an email draft in your device’s email application; nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. Selected attachments are not automatically added to that draft.

Why information may be used

Information may be used to operate the features you request, verify account details, respond to support requests, review submitted reports, and maintain or troubleshoot the service. The exact purposes and lawful bases must be confirmed for the regions where Lèi will be offered.

Sharing, storage, and retention

Some information may be processed by service providers that help deliver features such as messaging or report storage. The complete list of providers, hosting locations, access controls, retention periods, and deletion procedures is not yet specified in this draft and must be documented before publication.

Your choices and privacy requests

Review the account and profile controls available in the app. For a privacy question or request, contact the team through the Help Center. Applicable rights and the process and timing for requests need legal and operational review before this notice is finalized.

Changes and contact

This draft may change as Lèi’s features and data practices are confirmed. The public support address currently shown on the site is a placeholder and must be replaced with a real contact before launch.

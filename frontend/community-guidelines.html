Community Guidelines

Draft for review · October 2026

Draft, not final policy. These guidelines are a working draft for review, not a complete or currently enforceable policy. Please review them with the team before launch.

Our shared space

Lèi is intended to be a welcoming place for women to connect and support one another. Everyone who participates should help make the community respectful, inclusive, and safe.

How we ask members to participate

  • Treat other members with dignity, including when you disagree.
  • Respect people’s identities, boundaries, and decisions about what they choose to share.
  • Keep private conversations and personal information private. Get permission before sharing another person’s words, image, or identifying details elsewhere.
  • Share accurate information to the best of your knowledge, and be clear when something is an opinion or personal experience.
  • Use groups, events, and support spaces for their stated purpose and follow their additional participation rules.

Behavior that is not welcome

Harassment, threats, targeted abuse, hate, intimidation, impersonation, unwanted exposure of someone’s private information, and repeated unwanted contact are not compatible with the kind of community Lèi aims to build. Do not use the service to facilitate illegal activity, scams, or spam.

Reporting a concern

If something concerns you, use the available reporting controls or contact the team through the Help Center. Share relevant context, but avoid including passwords, verification codes, or information that is not needed to explain the concern.

Reports are intended for team review. The available report intake and review process may depend on service configuration. The Help Center does not currently provide case-status lookup.

Review and possible action

When tools and team capacity allow, reports may be reviewed and appropriate steps considered. Possible responses, review timelines, and appeal options have not yet been finalized; do not treat this draft as a promise of a particular outcome.

Urgent safety situations

Lèi is not an emergency response service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted local support resource.
